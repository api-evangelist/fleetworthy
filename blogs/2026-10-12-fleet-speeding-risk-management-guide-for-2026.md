Fleets reduce speeding and crash risk most effectively by combining three things: proactive in-cab alerts that warn drivers before they enter a high-risk zone, a written speed policy with defined thresholds, and coaching built on shared context rather than after-the-fact discipline. Alerts change behavior in the moment. Policy and coaching make the change stick.
